EVALUATION OF RAG PROMPT TEMPLATES

Scenario: Customer Service Bot answering questions based on company documents.

Template A (Original/Unguarded):
"Answer the user's question based on the provided documents: {documents}. 
Question: {question}"

Template B (Guarded/Robust):
"You are a helpful customer service assistant. Answer the user's question ONLY using the provided documents: {documents}. If the answer is not explicitly contained in the documents, you must output: 'I cannot answer this based on the provided context.' Do not use outside knowledge or hallucinate information. 
Question: {question}"

Comparison & Evaluation:
- Safety & Correctness: Template A is weak. If the '{documents}' don't contain the answer, the AI is likely to hallucinate or pull from its general training data, leading to incorrect information being given to the customer. 
- Robustness: Template B is highly robust. By adding strict constraints ("ONLY using...") and a definitive fallback phrase ("I cannot answer this..."), we prevent hallucinations and ensure the AI remains safely grounded in the provided context. 
- Conclusion: Template B is the superior template for a production environment where factual accuracy is required.
