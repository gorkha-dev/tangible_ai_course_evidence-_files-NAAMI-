Project: AI Transparency & Disclosure Application
Objective: Apply the 4-question disclosure test and establish clear ownership of AI-assisted work.

1. Task-level AI Use Judgment (Application):
- I actively applied the 4-question test (Would they act differently? Personally human? Consequential decision? The honest test) to evaluate recent AI-assisted tasks and determine if disclosure was necessary.
- For high-impact communications, such as performance summaries and sensitive client emails, I applied transparent disclosure.
- Implemented a standard signature protocol for AI-assisted outputs: "Drafted with AI assistance, reviewed and approved by me." This ensures full human accountability and maintains trust.
