Project: AI Output Policy & Grounding Framework
Objective: Mitigate hallucination risks and establish operational boundaries for AI utilization.

1. Hallucination Mitigation via Constraints (Grounding)
- Concept: AI systems must be explicitly constrained to rely solely on provided, verified data sources.
- Action: If the necessary facts are missing from the given context, the system is mandated to output a fallback response rather than attempting to predict or improvise answers.

2. Recognizing AI Outputs as Drafts
- Rule: AI-generated responses (e.g., customer service policies, data summaries) must be treated exclusively as preliminary drafts.
- Action: All outputs require mandatory human review and verification for accuracy, tone, and compliance before being finalized or shared externally to prevent legal and financial liability.
