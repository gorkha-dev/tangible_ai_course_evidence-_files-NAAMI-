Project: AI Verification & TRACE Protocol Master File
Objective: Establish a comprehensive framework for auditing AI outputs, verifying facts, and correcting hallucinations.

1. Fact-Checking (AI Output Verification):
- Action: All AI-generated facts must be systematically cross-referenced with verified source documents before approval. 

2. Identifying and Correcting Confident Incorrect AI Outputs:
- Action: Any confident AI claim lacking a direct source quote is immediately flagged as a potential hallucination and must be corrected by a human reviewer using the original data.

3. AI Output Verification (Trust but Verify):
- Rule: "Confidence is not evidence." We implement the TRACE auditing method (Sample outputs, Risk-weight sensitive data, force AI to Prove it with quotes, and Keep a trail) to ensure we verify AI outputs at scale without blindly trusting the machine.
